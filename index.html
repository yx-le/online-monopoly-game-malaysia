📜 Monopoly Tycoon Rules & Controls

Objective: Buy properties, collect rent, build houses & hotels, and bankrupt your opponents to become the last tycoon standing!

Rolling & Doubles: Roll two dice to advance clockwise around the 40-space perimeter. Rolling doubles grants a bonus roll, but rolling 3 consecutive doubles sends you directly to Jail.

Buying & Auctions: Land on an unowned property to buy it for its listed price, or pass to trigger a live bidding auction open to all solvent players.

Color Monopolies & Building: Own all streets of a single color group to charge double base rent on unimproved lots and unlock building Houses (up to 4) and a Hotel via Deeds & Build.

Mortgages & Debt: Mortgage unimproved properties for 50% of their price to raise cash. Unmortgaging costs 55% (50% + 10% interest). Mortgaged properties do not collect rent.

Board Inspection: Click any tile on the board at any time to view its full Title Deed rent schedule and ownership details.
